# YouTube Sponsorship Rate Card & Pricing Playbook

## 1. Valuation Formula
Pricing branded YouTube sponsorships should never be arbitrary. Agencies and brands look for view predictability.

### Step 1: Baseline Guaranteed Views
Calculate the trimmed median view count across the last 10 long-form uploads (drop the single highest viral outlier and single lowest outlier).

$$\text{Guaranteed Views} = \text{Median}(\text{Last 10 Videos})$$

### Step 2: Placement Multipliers
- **Integrated Mid-Roll (60–90 seconds)**: $1.0\times$ Baseline CPM
- **Integrated Pre-Roll (15–30 seconds)**: $0.7\times$ Baseline CPM
- **Dedicated Full Video (8–15 minutes)**: $3.0\times$ to $4.0\times$ Baseline CPM
- **Shorts / Vertical Clip (30–60 seconds)**: $0.3\times$ to $0.5\times$ Baseline CPM

### Step 3: Usage Rights & Exclusivity Premiums
- **Category Exclusivity (30 days)**: $+25\%$ to $+40\%$
- **Paid Whitelisting / Dark Ads (Meta/TikTok ads using creator handle)**: $+50\%$ per 30 days
- **Perpetual Organic YouTube Rights**: Included standard
- **First-Link-in-Description + Pinned Comment**: Standard inclusion

---

## 2. Package Bundling Strategies
Never sell single video integrations if you can sell packages:
- **Starter Test Pack**: 1 Dedicated Mid-Roll + 1 Community Tab Promo
- **Velocity Pack**: 3 Mid-Rolls across 60 days (10% discount)
- **Omnichannel Dominance Pack**: 3 Long-Form Mid-Rolls + 3 YouTube Shorts + 3 Newsletter Mentions (20% discount)

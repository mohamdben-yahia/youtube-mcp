# Scouting Filters & Velocity Scoring Matrix

## 1. Why Micro-Influencers (5k–50k) Outperform Mega-Creators
- **High Engagement Ratio**: Audiences under 50k have 3x–5x higher comment-per-view ratios than channels with 1M+ subscribers.
- **Cost Efficiency**: CPMs for creators under 50k subs are often $20–$35 vs. $80–$150 for established legacy creators.
- **Algorithmic Upside**: If a breakout video surges to 250k views, a sponsor who paid for a 15k-view baseline gets an astronomical 15x return on investment.

---

## 2. The 3 Red Flags of Inflated Channels
When scouting channels, filter out accounts exhibiting these pathological patterns:
1. **Shorts-Inflated Vanity Subs**: 200,000 subscribers, but long-form videos only get 800 views. (Audience does not convert).
2. **Zombie Subscriptions**: Channel was started in 2017, has 100k subscribers, but average views over the last 90 days are under 2,000.
3. **Sub-for-Sub / Giveaway Spikes**: Sudden subscriber leaps with zero corresponding video comments.

# Retention & Pacing Benchmarks for High-Growth Creators

## 1. The Critical Retention Moments
- **0:00 – 0:30 (The Hook Window)**: Target $> 70\%$ retention at 30 seconds.
  - *Failure Mode*: Channel intro logos, asking for subscriptions, rambling greetings.
  - *Fix*: State the outcome in the first 5 seconds. Show proof or end result immediately.
- **0:30 – 2:00 (The Setup Window)**: Target $> 60\%$ retention at 2 minutes.
  - *Failure Mode*: Abstract theory without real-world application.
  - *Fix*: Deliver a quick early win before starting the complex material.
- **2:00 – End (The Flow Window)**: Minimize gradual slope decay.
  - *Failure Mode*: Monotone explaining stretches $>45$ seconds.
  - *Fix*: Pattern interrupts every 30–45s (zoom cut, b-roll, screen highlight, audio cue).

## 2. Words-Per-Minute (WPM) Benchmarks
- **Under 120 WPM**: Too slow for modern YouTube audiences; viewer perceives dragging pacing and leaves.
- **135 – 155 WPM**: The sweet spot for conversational, authoritative educational content (e.g., Ali Abdaal, MKBHD).
- **160 – 180+ WPM**: High-energy tech tutorials and fast-paced commentary (e.g., Fireship).

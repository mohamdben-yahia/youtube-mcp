# Title Psychology & CTR Engineering Rules

## 1. The 5 Core Click Drivers
Every 80+ CTR title utilizes at least 2 of these psychological levers:
1. **The Curiosity Gap**: Create tension that can only be resolved by watching (e.g., *"The 1 Line of Code That Broke Google"*).
2. **High Stakes / Threat Avoidance**: Humans are 2x more motivated to avoid loss than to gain benefits (e.g., *"Stop Writing Python Like This in 2026"*).
3. **Extreme Specificity**: Concrete numbers build credibility (e.g., *"How I Built a $12,400/mo SaaS with No Employees"*).
4. **Counter-Intuitive Contrast**: Juxtapose two opposing concepts (e.g., *"Why the Worst Programmers Make the Best Founders"*).
5. **Speed to Outcome**: Promise rapid payoff (e.g., *"Learn SQL in 18 Minutes"*).

---

## 2. Character Length & Mobile Truncation
- YouTube mobile home feed truncates titles at **50 to 55 characters**.
- Always place the primary hook and emotional trigger in the first 40 characters.
- Never place important keywords at the very end of a 90-character title.

---

## 3. The Thumbnail Complement Rule
- **Rule**: Never repeat the title verbatim inside the thumbnail text.
- Title and Thumbnail must form a **Visual Conversation**:
  - *Title*: *"I Tried Quitting Coding for 30 Days"*
  - *Thumbnail Text*: *"NEVER AGAIN."* (Shows character looking exhausted at a whiteboard).

# Algorithmic Publishing Windows & Indexing Protocols

## 1. The YouTube Indexing Lag Effect
When a video is uploaded directly as **Public**, it enters the recommendation system at a disadvantage:
1. **High-Definition Processing**: 1080p and 4K transcodes take 15 to 90 minutes. Early viewers receiving 360p resolution drop off within 10 seconds.
2. **Auto-Captioning & Semantic Entity Extraction**: YouTube's natural language models listen to spoken audio to classify search tags and topic categorization. This process takes 60 to 120 minutes.
3. **Content ID Scanning**: Copyright and ad-suitability scans run in the background.

### Standard Operating Procedure: The 3-Hour Unlisted Buffer
1. Upload video 3 hours before targeted public release as **Unlisted**.
2. Set thumbnail, video title, description, and end screens.
3. Confirm 1080p/4K transcode is complete and green monetization dollar sign is active.
4. Schedule publication for the calculated Sweet Spot window.

---

## 2. Peak Hours vs. Sweet Spot Windows
- **Peak Consumption**: When the largest number of viewers in the geographic audience are actively watching YouTube (e.g. 7:00 PM – 10:00 PM local time).
- **Sweet Spot Publishing**: 2 to 3 hours *before* Peak Consumption (e.g. 2:00 PM – 4:00 PM).
  - Allows early notification distribution to passionate core subscribers.
  - Generates initial high retention velocity before the broader casual audience opens their YouTube app.

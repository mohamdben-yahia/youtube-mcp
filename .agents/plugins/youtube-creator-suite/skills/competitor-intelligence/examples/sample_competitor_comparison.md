# Head-to-Head Competitor Intelligence Benchmark

**Vertical**: Freelance Web Development & Productivity  
**Competitors Analyzed**: `@creator_alpha` vs. `@creator_beta`

---

### Comparative Performance Matrix

| Metric | Creator Alpha (`@creator_alpha`) | Creator Beta (`@creator_beta`) | Advantage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Subscribers** | 120,000 | 45,000 | Alpha ($2.6\times$) |
| **Median Views (Last 5)** | 18,500 | 28,200 | **Beta ($1.52\times$)** |
| **Upload Cadence** | 3 videos / week | 1 video / 10 days | Beta (Higher leverage) |
| **Avg Video Duration** | 6:40 | 14:20 | Beta (Higher watch time) |
| **Primary Monetization** | AdSense + Cheap Merch | Skool Community ($49/mo) | **Beta ($35k/mo est.)** |
| **Active Sponsors** | Hostinger, Skillshare | Warp Terminal, Linear | Beta (High-tier tech) |

### Key Takeaway & Counter-Strategy
While Creator Alpha has 2.6x more subscribers, Creator Beta is dramatically outperforming on view velocity and backend monetization. Creator Beta's audience is enterprise/freelancer-focused rather than casual students. To win in this niche: model Creator Beta's in-depth 14-minute format, but exploit Creator Beta's lack of beginner roadmaps.

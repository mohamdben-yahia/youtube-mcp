# Competitor Funnel Anatomy & Reverse Engineering

## 1. Deconstructing the Description Box
A creator's YouTube description box reveals their entire business model. Inspect the first 5 lines and bottom footer:

1. **Lines 1–3 (Above the "Show More" fold)**:
   - Primary Conversion Objective.
   - If it links to a free resource (Lead Magnet) $\rightarrow$ Email-first creator selling high-ticket backend courses/consulting.
   - If it links directly to a sponsor coupon $\rightarrow$ Sponsor-dependent creator.
   - If it links to GitHub/Docs $\rightarrow$ Open-source or developer relations profile.

2. **Affiliate Tool Stacks**:
   - Recurring software links (e.g. Notion, Webflow, NordVPN, Epidemic Sound).
   - Generates predictable passive monthly recurring revenue (MRR).

---

## 2. Head-to-Head Benchmark Metrics
When comparing 2 to 5 rival channels:
- **Upload Efficiency**: $\frac{\text{Total 30-day views}}{\text{Uploads per month}}$. A channel doing 200k views on 2 uploads is outperforming a channel doing 250k views on 15 uploads.
- **Audience Loyalty**: Ratio of comments to views. Standard benchmark is 1 comment per 200–400 views. Anything above 1:150 indicates fanatic community engagement.

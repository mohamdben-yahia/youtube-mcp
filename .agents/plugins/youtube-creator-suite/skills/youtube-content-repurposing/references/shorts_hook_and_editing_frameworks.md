# Shorts Hook & Repurposing Frameworks

## 1. The 3-Second Visual Hook in Vertical Video
- In YouTube Shorts, $>60\%$ of viewers swipe away within the first 2 seconds.
- **Rule 1: Never Start With Context**: Never say *"Today I want to talk about..."* or *"In this video..."*.
- **Rule 2: Start Mid-Action / Bold Statement**: Start on the most provocative sentence of the segment (e.g., *"This one line of code will delete your entire database if you aren't careful"*).
- **Rule 3: On-Screen Text**: 70% of mobile users watch with sound down initially. The opening hook must be displayed as bold, contrasting subtitles in the top third of the screen.

---

## 2. Converting Shorts Viewers to Long-Form Subscribers
- Shorts audiences are notoriously low-retention for long-form content.
- Use YouTube's native **"Related Video"** feature:
  - Link the Short directly to the full-length parent video.
  - Deliver 80% of the payoff in the Short, and tease the deep technical demo in the Related Video.

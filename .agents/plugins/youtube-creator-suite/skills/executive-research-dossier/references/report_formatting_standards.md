# Executive Report Formatting Standards

## Structure of an Institutional-Grade YouTube Intelligence Report

### Section 1: Executive Summary & Market Thesis
- Maximum 250 words.
- Highlight the **TAM (Total Addressable Attention)**, estimated niche CPM ranges, and the single largest unserved demand vector.

### Section 2: Competitive Positioning Matrix
Always present competitors in a structured Markdown table:
- Channel Name & URL
- Audience Tier (Micro, Mid, Macro)
- Upload Cadence (Weekly, Bi-weekly, Sporadic)
- Outlier Performance Multiplier
- Monetization Mix (AdSense %, Brand Deals %, Owned Products %)

### Section 3: Algorithmic Content Vectors
- Top 3 recurring hooks that generate $>3\times$ average views.
- Top 3 viewer friction points extracted from sentiment mining.

### Section 4: 90-Day Execution Roadmap
- Sequential, week-by-week implementation plan with concrete deliverables.
